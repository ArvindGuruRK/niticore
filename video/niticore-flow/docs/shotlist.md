**Idea (unchanged).** One unbroken flight through a field of light. Particles carry the story from
shape to shape; every component on screen is a real Niticore component, built out of particles,
resolved crisp, then poured into the next as a twisting stream. Nothing cuts.

**v1 → v2 (client changes).**
1. The India claim is gone. New opening: "The world is accelerating with AI." / "Now, AI needs
   governance built for what's next." / "Introducing Niticore."
2. No ring or orbit behind the wordmark, at the open or the end. The wordmark itself does more:
   letters assemble one by one from their particles, a light sweep crosses them, the sparkles spin in.
3. The network-and-shockwave problem section becomes a 3D AI-agent avatar: a glossy blob with eyes
   that breathes with the voice, buds into small agent blobs, and acts out each line.
4. Frameworks: six cards (EU AI Act, ISO/IEC 42001, NIST AI RMF, UAE PDPL, ADGM DPR 2021, DIFC
   Regulation 10), exactly as the site's FrameworkCards, under "Align with governance frameworks such as…".
5. Removed: "Guardrails for every agent" (agent switches, shield, blocked rogue action) and "Watch over
   everything" (runtime monitoring). Kept: "People in the loop" with the approval UI.
6. New: the five-stage loop (Discover, Assess, Govern, Prove, Monitor) as the site's OrbitSteps ring.
7. New: "Six ways governance actually gets enforced." with a product demo for each of the six.
8. The constellation shows every component, the new ones included.
9. End card: the niticore-sub logo ("AI Governance. From Policy to Proof."), www.niticore.ai, no ring.
