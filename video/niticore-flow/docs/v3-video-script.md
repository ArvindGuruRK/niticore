## 3. The new parts, scene by scene

### 3.1 Built for six industries (1:45 – 2:02)

**The idea.** A stage for each industry: its name, huge, on the left; on the right, a 3D sculpture of
its world made of particles, the same light as the rest of the film, turning slowly on a glowing
pedestal. As the voice names each industry, the particles flow and morph into the next world.

| Time | Voiceover | On screen |
|------|-----------|-----------|
| 1:45 – 1:50 | "Built for industries where AI errors carry real consequences, such as…" | The last demo pours into a globe of light on a pedestal, turning slowly |
| 1:50 | "…finance," | **01 / 06 · Finance** · the globe morphs into a 3D city of towers with a market line rising over it to an arrow |
| 1:52 | "healthcare," | **02 / 06 · Healthcare** · a 3D heart, an ECG trace running in front of it, a medical cross floating beside |
| 1:53 | "workforce," | **03 / 06 · Workforce** · five people, joined by arcs of light that carry pulses between them |
| 1:55 | "government," | **04 / 06 · Government** · a capitol: steps, columns, pediment, dome and a waving flag |
| 1:56 | "retail and consumer," | **05 / 06 · Retail & consumer** · a shopping bag and a cart, a price tag swinging from the handle |
| 1:58 | "and infrastructure and mobility." | **06 / 06 · Infrastructure & mobility** · a suspension bridge, a train crossing it, water rippling below |
| 2:00 – 2:02 | — | The particles return to the globe; the six names line up on the left. **"Six industries and more. One governance practice."** |

### 3.2 The regulatory wave (2:08 – 2:42)

**The idea.** The headline says "the regulatory wave", so we show a real wave: a wide, rolling sea of
light that rises with every year and peaks at the last milestone. The timeline rides its crest, and
the camera rides along it from 2024 to 2027.

**What it looks like.** A line of light along the crest (solid up to today, dashed beyond it), a green
comet that reaches each milestone as the voice names it, and each milestone igniting with a burst of
light: the date rolls up, the label writes on, the node fills. A huge, faint year rolls like an
odometer behind the wave (2024 → 2025 → 2026 → 2027). A "Today" marker lands on the line between
January 2026 and January 2027.

## 5. Look, sound and voice

- **Look.** The same style as the approved film: a dark background and light particles, the Niticore
  colours (green only for the one key word or action, violet for icons and illustrations), and
  sentence case (no all-caps), at a medium, unhurried pace.
- **Music.** The same song continues: a lighter groove under the industries with a bell on each
  name, the full chorus for "innovate with confidence", a slow swell under the wave that climbs a step
  with each milestone, a ticking clock under the countdown, the chorus again for the call to action,
  and the original logo chord at the end. Mastered to -14 LUFS.

## 6. Notes

1. **EU AI Act dates.** The timeline uses the EU's current dates. The GPAI rules have applied since
   August 2025. After the "Digital Omnibus" (in force 27 July 2026), the Annex III high-risk rules
   apply from 2 December 2027, which is also the countdown's date.
   Sources: [Jones Walker](https://www.joneswalker.com/en/insights/blogs/ai-law-blog/yes-august-2-still-matters-the-eu-approved-a-high-risk-ai-delay-but-most-trans.html?id=102nbon) ·
   [Gibson Dunn](https://www.gibsondunn.com/eu-ai-act-omnibus-agreement-postponed-high-risk-deadlines-and-other-key-changes/)
2. **The day count** is exact for each expo day (two files: 422 on 6 October, 421 on 7 October). For
   use after the expo, any other day's file (or a date-only version) can be made from the same film.
3. **Left out from the investor slide:** "The category is being formed right now." and the "wins
   the decade" line, since they speak to investors rather than customers.
