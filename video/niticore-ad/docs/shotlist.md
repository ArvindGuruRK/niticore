**Idea.** A software product ad in a grid world. The grid opens on India's claim, builds the
wordmark tile by tile, fills with AI systems (blocks extruded from cells, data packets on the
lines), loses control in the shadows, then a scan line brings clarity and the Niticore app rises
out of the floor as a 3D glass window. A cursor runs a real demo inside it. The world lights up,
and the wordmark returns.

**Look.** Deep ink (#030308 → #0B1028), blue grid light, HDR bloom with anamorphic streaks, faint
dust only (no particle effects). Sora (display) + Manrope (UI). Green is the brand and the action
colour (logo, primary buttons); teal for success, coral for risk, amber for shadow AI, violet for
approvals. The app UI follows the real platform's structure (sidebar: Dashboard, Use Cases, Risk
Dashboard, LLM Evaluations, Assessment, Audit Dashboard, Policy Dashboard, My Task) in the
film's own dark glass style; it is drawn, not a screenshot.
