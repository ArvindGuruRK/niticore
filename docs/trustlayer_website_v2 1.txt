Regulatory coverage
Every framework your AI needs to comply with.
TrustLayer models the clause structure, article hierarchy, and enforcement context of each framework natively — not as a generic checklist overlay.
EU AI Act
EU AI Act 2024/1689
The world's first comprehensive horizontal AI law. Includes high-risk classification, conformity assessment, FRIA workflows, transparency obligations, and GPAI provisions for foundation models. High-risk enforcement is live as of August 2026.
ISO 42001
ISO/IEC 42001:2023
The international standard for AI Management Systems. TrustLayer's team is ISO 42001-certified. Coverage spans context, risk treatment, performance evaluation, and continual improvement — with audit-ready evidence at every clause.
NIST AI RMF
NIST AI RMF 1.0
The US voluntary framework for managing AI risk across GOVERN, MAP, MEASURE, and MANAGE functions. Widely adopted in US federal agencies and financial services. Cross-mapped to EU AI Act obligations for maximum evidence reuse.
GDPR
GDPR & Data Protection
Data Protection Impact Assessments, automated decision-making controls, and data minimisation requirements — linked to the same evidence base as your AI governance controls. Cross-mapped to UAE PDPL and DIFC DPL for dual-jurisdiction organisations.
UAE · DIFC · ADGM
UAE Regulatory Suite
DIFC Regulation 10 (enforcement live), UAE PDPL (federal deadline January 2027), and ADGM DPR 2021 — each modelled separately. Mainland, DIFC, and ADGM carry materially different obligations. TrustLayer is the only platform that maps all three distinctly.
UAE — the fastest-moving AI regulation market
The UAE AI clock is already running.
DIFC Regulation 10 is in active enforcement. The UAE PDPL federal deadline is January 2027. Organisations operating across mainland UAE, DIFC, and ADGM face three materially different obligation sets — and most governance platforms treat them as one. TrustLayer doesn't.
DIFC Regulation 10 — enforcement active since January 2026. AI governance frameworks are now a regulatory requirement for DIFC-registered entities, not a best practice. UAE PDPL federal deadline: January 2027.
Talk to our UAE team →
UAE Mainland
Federal · UAE PDPL · January 2027
Federal Personal Data Protection Law with a January 2027 compliance deadline. Data processing registration, consent requirements, and cross-border transfer restrictions — cross-mapped from GDPR controls to minimise duplication for global organisations.
DIFC
Dubai International Financial Centre · Enforcement active
DIFC Regulation 10 requires a board-approved AI governance framework, AI Impact Assessments, and a designated AI Systems Officer. TrustLayer includes the ASO role natively and automates Impact Assessment workflows for registered AI systems.
ADGM
Abu Dhabi Global Market · FSRA regulated
ADGM DPR 2021 closely mirrors UK GDPR in structure. If you're already GDPR-compliant, TrustLayer maps your existing evidence across to ADGM obligations automatically — dramatically reducing the compliance overhead for Abu Dhabi-registered entities.
