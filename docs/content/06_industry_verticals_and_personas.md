## 2. Multi-Stakeholder Alignment (Persona Views)

AI governance is inherently cross-functional. NitiCore avoids one-size-fits-all UIs by providing role-tailored dashboards and workflows:

### 1. Board of Directors & Chief Executive Officer (CEO)
* **Core Question**: *"Can we trust our AI, and are we legally protected?"*
* **What NitiCore Provides**:
  * Executive Governance Readiness™ health scorecard.
  * Brand reputation risk indicators and exposure heat maps.
  * Assurance that high-risk systems comply with EU AI Act and regional directives.

### 2. Chief AI Officer (CAIO), CTO & CIO
* **Core Question**: *"Can we scale AI deployment velocity without breaking compliance?"*
* **What NitiCore Provides**:
  * Unified enterprise AI system inventory (models, agents, third-party APIs).
  * DevSecOps-native governance gates embedded directly into CI/CD pipelines.
  * Lifecycle monitoring tracking accuracy drift, latency, and agent tool execution.

### 3. Chief Risk Officer (CRO) & Risk Managers
* **Core Question**: *"Where are our aggregate exposures, and are controls actually working?"*
* **What NitiCore Provides**:
  * Multi-dimensional risk quantification (inherent vs. residual risk).
  * Continuous control effectiveness telemetry.
  * Automated escalation of unmitigated high-risk use cases.

### 4. General Counsel & Chief Compliance Officer (CCO)
* **Core Question**: *"Can we prove compliance immediately when a regulator audits us?"*
* **What NitiCore Provides**:
  * Immutable, timestamped audit logs of all approvals and model changes.
  * Pre-built templates for Declarations of Conformity, FRIAs, and DPIAs.
  * One-click regulator-ready evidence dossier exports.

### 5. Data Protection Officer (DPO)
* **Core Question**: *"Are training datasets compliant with GDPR and UAE PDPL data rights?"*
* **What NitiCore Provides**:
  * Data lineage and consent tracking for model training corpora.
  * Automated Article 22 automated decision-making controls and opt-out logs.
  * Cross-mapping from GDPR to UAE PDPL and DIFC Data Protection Law.

### 6. AI Engineers & Data Scientists
* **Core Question**: *"Will governance slow down our deployment sprints?"*
* **What NitiCore Provides**:
  * Clean, API-driven registration workflows; no bureaucratic paperwork.
  * Pre-configured test suites for bias detection, prompt injection, and hallucination.
  * Immediate clarity on required evidence before model freeze.

### 7. Internal & External Auditors
* **Core Question**: *"Show me the verifiable evidence trail."*
* **What NitiCore Provides**:
  * Dedicated, read-only auditor inspection portals.
  * Complete provenance chains from statutory requirement to verified code artifact.
  * Historical versioning of all model architectures, parameters, and policies.
