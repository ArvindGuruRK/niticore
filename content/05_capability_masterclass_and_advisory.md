### The 16-Module Master Curriculum

1. **AI Governance Fundamentals**: Definitions, core principles, and the AI lifecycle.
2. **EU AI Act in Practice**: High-risk Annex III classification, conformity, and Article 27 FRIA.
3. **ISO/IEC 42001:2023**: Designing and certifying an Artificial Intelligence Management System (AIMS).
4. **NIST AI RMF 1.0**: Operationalizing GOVERN, MAP, MEASURE, and MANAGE.
5. **AI Risk Management & Quantification**: Scoring probabilistic systems vs deterministic code.
6. **Responsible AI & Ethics**: Algorithmic fairness, transparency, explainability, and accountability.
7. **AI Lifecycle Governance**: From prototype to production monitoring and retirement.
8. **Agentic AI & Autonomous Systems**: Tool use, runtime boundaries, and kill-switch mechanisms.
9. **Policies & Control Frameworks**: Writing enforceable, automated AI policies.
10. **UAE & GCC Regulatory Suite**: DIFC Regulation 10, UAE PDPL, and ADGM FSRA mandates.
11. **Governance Operating Models**: RACI matrices, AI Ethics Committees, and role definition.
12. **Board & C-Suite Reporting**: Metrics that convey real risk without technical jargon.
13. **Third-Party AI & Supply Chain**: Auditing SaaS vendors, foundation models, and external APIs.
14. **LLM Evaluation & Red Teaming**: Stress-testing, hallucination detection, and jailbreak prevention.
15. **Data Governance for AI**: Training data provenance, copyright compliance, and synthetic data validation.
16. **Maturity & Continuous Improvement**: Progressing from Level 1 Emerging to Level 5 Adaptive.

> *"From policy to practice. Expert-led. Implementation-focused."*

### Four Core Practice Areas

#### 01 · STRATEGY
* Establish the enterprise AI Governance vision, risk appetite, and strategic objectives.
* Map current and upcoming regulatory exposure across all operating regions.
* Design the organizational governance structure, AI committee charter, and RACI roles.

#### 02 · FRAMEWORK & POLICY DESIGN
* Author tailored policy suites covering acceptable AI use, model validation, and third-party procurement.
* Design multi-framework control libraries harmonized across ISO 42001, EU AI Act, NIST, and UAE mandates.
* Formulate custom risk classification rubrics and assessment criteria.

#### 03 · IMPLEMENTATION & OPERATIONALIZATION
* Configure NitiCore platform workflows, automated approval gates, and integrations with CI/CD and MLOps tools.
* Embed governance checkpoints directly into JIRA, ServiceNow, GitHub, and enterprise ticketing systems.
* Deliver change management, internal champion training, and organizational handover.

#### 04 · INDEPENDENT ASSURANCE & CERTIFICATION
* Conduct formal gap analyses and external audit dry-runs.
* Prepare enterprises for accredited ISO/IEC 42001 certification audits.
* Provide ongoing regulatory intelligence monitoring and quarterly executive posture reviews.
