## 2. Global Framework Deep Dives

### A. European Union AI Act (Regulation 2024/1689)
* **Status**: Global benchmark; high-risk enforcement active as of August 2026.
* **NitiCore Capabilities**:
  * **Annex III Risk Classifier**: Determines whether systems fall under critical infrastructure, education, employment, access to essential services, law enforcement, or biometric categorization.
  * **Conformity Assessments**: Guides internal and third-party conformity evaluation workflows.
  * **FRIA (Fundamental Rights Impact Assessment)**: Built-in assessment templates satisfying Article 27 obligations.
  * **GPAI & Foundation Models**: Specialized tracking for General Purpose AI models and systemic risk mitigation.
  * **Technical Documentation**: Auto-generates compliant Annex IV technical files ready for market surveillance authorities.

### B. ISO/IEC 42001:2023 (Artificial Intelligence Management System - AIMS)
* **Status**: The premier international certifiable standard for AI organizational governance.
* **NitiCore Capabilities**:
  * Direct clause-by-clause mapping (Clauses 4 through 10) covering context, leadership, planning, support, operations, and evaluation.
  * Annex A & B control libraries embedded natively into approval workflows.
  * Internal audit dry-run modules and certification readiness benchmarking.

### C. NIST AI Risk Management Framework (NIST AI RMF 1.0)
* **Status**: The standard of choice for US enterprise, federal agencies, and global technology providers.
* **NitiCore Capabilities**:
  * Core functional grouping: **GOVERN**, **MAP**, **MEASURE**, and **MANAGE**.
  * Dynamic cross-mapping between NIST sub-categories and EU AI Act articles for transatlantic operations.

### D. Data Protection & Privacy (GDPR, UK GDPR)
* **NitiCore Capabilities**:
  * Integrated **Data Protection Impact Assessment (DPIA)** workflows.
  * Direct controls for Article 22 (Automated individual decision-making, including profiling).
  * Data lineage, minimization, and privacy-preserving model verification.
